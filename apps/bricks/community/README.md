# Community Bricks

Bricks made by the community and reviewed by Kiln's maintainers. Each one is a
`kiln.brick/v2` document in a folder named after its author:

```text
community/<author>/<id>.json
```

To submit one, build it in the [Brick creator](https://kiln.site/bricks/new),
download the JSON, and open a pull request that adds it here. A reviewer checks
that it runs what it says it runs, pins images to a tag, and asks for nothing it
doesn't use. Merging is the approval: kiln.site lists it under Community, and
it's published in the community catalog.

Community Bricks are reviewed, not maintained, by Kiln. Their authors keep them
working.
